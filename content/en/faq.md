---
items:
  - question: My dog never sits still. Is that a problem?
    answer: No — zoomies welcome. Movement photographs better than posing, and for calm portraits we simply shoot after the energy is gone, not before.
  - question: Where do sessions take place?
    answer: In and around Brandenburg — outdoors or at your home. Berlin works too.
  - question: Can you photograph cats too?
    answer: Yes, ideally at home where they feel safe. Cats cooperate on their own schedule; I plan for that.
  - question: Do you speak dog?
    answer: Fließend. Cat as well, though cats pretend not to understand German. Or English. Or you.
  - question: What if the weather is terrible?
    answer: We reschedule free of charge or move indoors. Overcast light is excellent; drizzle plus a dramatic dog is even better.
  - question: How should I prepare my pet?
    answer: A walk for energetic dogs, favourite treats, a toy. No bath required — scruffy photographs well.
  - question: When do I get my photos?
    answer: A sneak peek within a few days, the full gallery within two weeks.
