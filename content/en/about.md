Hallo! I'm Fia, a photographer from Brandenburg. I photograph pets as they actually are: the head tilt, the full-speed chaos, the guilty look beside a suspiciously empty treat bag. Ein kleines Fiasko is part of most sessions — hence the name.

The method is simple. We go where your pet is comfortable, I bring Leckerlis and patience, and nobody says "Sitz!" unless they mean it. The best photos happen between the poses.
