items:
  - question: My dog never sits still. Is that a problem?
    answer: Not at all — zoomies are half the fun. Motion shots are some of my favourites, and for calmer portraits we simply wait for the post-zoomie nap. Ordnung is overrated anyway.
  - question: Where do sessions take place?
    answer: Anywhere in and around Brandenburg — forests, fields, lakesides, city corners or your own home. Berlin is easily doable too. If your pet loves it, it's a good location.
  - question: Can you photograph cats too?
    answer: Absolutely — cats are a speciality. Cat sessions usually happen at home, where they feel safe and can ignore me in comfort until curiosity wins.
  - question: Do you speak dog?
    answer: "Fließend. Cat as well, although cats pretend not to understand German. Or English. Or you."
  - question: What if the weather is terrible?
    answer: "We reschedule for free, or switch to a cosy at-home session. Overcast skies actually make beautiful soft light — and light rain plus a dramatic dog is a great combination. Sehr dramatisch. Sehr schön."
  - question: How should I prepare my pet?
    answer: A walk beforehand for energetic dogs, their favourite treats and a toy — that's it. Please don't wash the personality out of them; scruffy is photogenic.
  - question: When do I get my photos?
    answer: A sneak peek within a few days, and the full edited gallery within two weeks of the session.
