Hallo! I'm Fia — photographer, cat whisperer and firm believer that every pet is secretly a supermodel. A dramatic one. Ein richtiges Fiasko, sometimes — hence the name.

I grew up in Brandenburg surrounded by animals, and somewhere between chasing cats through the garden and bribing dogs with Leckerlis I discovered that the best portraits happen when nobody says "Sitz!". My camera and I specialise in the in-between moments: the head tilt, the mid-zoomie chaos, the guilty look next to the suspiciously empty treat bag.

They say we Germans love Ordnung. My sessions have none — on purpose. We take our time, we follow your pet's lead, and we laugh a lot. The fiasco is part of the plan; that's where the good photos live.
